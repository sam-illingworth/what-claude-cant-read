# Home
## Warsan Shire

**Publication:** First collected in *Bless the Daughter Raised by a Voice in Her Head* (Penguin Random House, 2022). The poem circulated widely online from approximately 2009 and gained global prominence during the 2015 European refugee crisis. It was never formally published before the 2022 collection.

**Where to read:** [Poetry International](https://www.poetryinternational.com/en/poets-poems/poems/poem/103-30925_HOME)
